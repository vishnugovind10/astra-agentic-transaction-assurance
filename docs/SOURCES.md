## Source-use rules

- Distinguish official specifications from marketing statements and reported experiments.
- Do not infer that a product capability is live merely because it appears on a roadmap.
- Do not claim standard conformance from an adapter or field mapping.
- Do not claim absence of competitors based on a limited scan.
- Record the exact specification version and retrieval date in implementation documentation.
